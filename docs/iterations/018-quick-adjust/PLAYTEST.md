# Mission 018 playtest — Quick Adjust

About 10 minutes, on the Kamvas with the pen, at your usual 150 %.

## 1. Hold B (4 min)

1. Hover the pen over the drawing and **hold B**. After a moment a small card appears above the pen.
2. With B still held, drag the **Size** slider with the pen, then **Opacity** and **Smoothing**. Are the numbers
   easy to read while you drag?
3. Still holding B, drag **anywhere** outside the card, left and right: the size follows. The ring shows the real size.
4. Let go of B: the card closes and the brush keeps the new settings. Draw a stroke to check.
5. Hold B again, change something, and press **Esc** before letting go: everything goes back.
6. Tap B quickly: it should just pick the brush, with no card.

## 2. Hold E and Z (3 min)

1. **Hold E:** the eraser's size and smoothing, the same way.
2. **Hold Z** over a detail and drag anywhere up or right: it zooms in around the spot under the pen. Down or left zooms
   out. Let go to stay there, or press Esc to go back.

## 3. The size above the pen (2 min)

1. **Right-drag** on the canvas (or with the pen's button) to resize the brush: the size should show above the pen,
   not under it.
2. **Right-click** for the quick palette and drag its centre: the size also shows above the pen while you drag.

## What to tell me

- Is the hold delay right? Is the card where you want it, and big enough to hit with the pen?
- Would you rather it worked differently: other keys, more or fewer rows, or a different look?
- Anything that gets in the way while you draw.
