# Palette attribution

DoodleNook bundles ten palettes made by other artists, in the **Lospec** collection of the palette library. Each one is
bundled only because its creator (or rights holder) has said it may be used freely. Lospec itself publishes no licence
for palettes, and a palette being free to download doesn't mean it may be redistributed, so palettes whose terms are
unclear are **not** bundled. They are listed further down with how to use them yourself.

How the data was made (Mission 012, 21 November 2026):

- Each palette's colours were copied from its own Lospec download (`https://lospec.com/palette-list/<name>.json`). They are
  in the published order, and each list was checked against the page's "Number of colors". No colour was read from a
  picture or typed by hand.
- Every quotation below was checked word for word against the saved source page.
- DoodleNook never contacts Lospec. The palettes are part of the program, and Lospec's terms forbid scraping its site.

The Palettes window shows each bundled palette's creator, terms and source. Crediting is optional for all of them, but
DoodleNook credits them anyway.

  Twitter account.

Moods covered: bright and playful (PICO-8, ARQ16), vibrant general purpose (Resurrect 64), muted and earthy
(DawnBringer 16, Japanese Woodblock), soft and warm (CL8UDS, AYY4), cold and gloomy (Cave4), horror and night
(A Blood-Steeped Corridor), and 1-bit (1bit Monitor Glow).

Sizes: 2, 4, 5, 8, 12, 16 and 64 colours. No 24- or 32-colour palette by another artist was found with clear
permission. DoodleNook's own **Nook 32** covers 32 colours.

## Requested but not bundled

None of these palettes has a licence or a statement by its creator that allows redistribution. Lospec offers each as
a free download, so you can still use them in your own pictures:

1. Open the palette's page on Lospec.
2. Download the **HEX** or **GPL** file.
3. In DoodleNook, open **Palettes… › Import…**. The palette lands in *Imported* with its exact colours and works with
   Palette Lock, Remap and Game Edit like any other.
